## Executive Summary

**Rently** is a zero-brokerage rental property platform connecting tenants directly with verified owners across Maharashtra, India. Built by **Arynoxtech**, Rently combines AI-powered search, legally binding e-contracts with Aadhaar eSign, Trust Score verification, and secure payments — all in a mobile-first experience.

**The problem**: India's ₹20B+ rental market is plagued by brokers charging 1-2 months' rent as commission, fake listings, deposit disputes, and no legal documentation. 78% of tenants in Maharashtra report negative experiences with brokers (ANAROCK 2024).

**The solution**: Rently eliminates brokers, uses AI to detect scams, generates legally valid contracts, and provides end-to-end rental management — at a fraction of the cost.

**Ask**: ₹2 Cr Seed Round
**Use of funds**: Product development, Maharashtra expansion, growth marketing
**Target**: 100K active listings, 50K monthly active users within 18 months

## How It Works

### For Tenants
1. **Search** — AI-powered search with budget, location, commute time, BHK preferences
2. **Verify** — Trust Score (A+ to F) shows owner reliability, deposit compliance, photo authenticity
3. **Chat** — Direct messaging with owners, no broker interference
4. **Visit** — Schedule visits, get directions via live map with nearby POI
5. **Sign** — AI generates legally valid Maharashtra-format contract, eSign via Aadhaar OTP
6. **Pay** — Secure rent payments via Razorpay, track deposit compliance
7. **Live** — Ongoing tenancy management, maintenance requests

### For Owners
1. **List** — AI-assisted listing with auto-pricing suggestions based on market data
2. **Verify** — Trust Score boosts visibility, verification badge increases inquiries 3x
3. **Manage** — Dashboard with views, inquiries, analytics, price comparison
4. **Contract** — Generate e-contracts at ₹50 (vs ₹399-2K market rate)
5. **Collect** — Secure rent collection with automated reminders
6. **Scale** — Subscription plans for multiple property management

### Trust Score System (7 Factors)
1. **Owner Verification** (20%) — ID, phone, email verified
2. **Deposit Compliance** (15%) — Within Maharashtra's 2-month cap
3. **Photo Quality** (10%) — Authentic, multiple photos
4. **Listing Freshness** (15%) — Recently updated
5. **Scam Risk** (20%) — AI detects 10 scam patterns
6. **Listing Quality** (10%) — Complete description, amenities
7. **Price Reasonableness** (10%) — Compared to market rates

### AI Scam Detection (10 Rules)
1. Unusually low price vs market average
2. Deposit exceeding legal cap
3. No property images
4. Unverified owner
5. Duplicate listing detection
6. Very new listing with suspicious patterns
7. No phone number provided
8. Round-number pricing (₹10,000 exactly)
9. Above-market pricing
10. Stale listing with no updates
