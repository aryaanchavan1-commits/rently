## Description
Nivasa by Arynoxtech is Maharashtra's trusted zero-brokerage rental platform. Find your next home directly from verified owners — no middlemen, no hidden fees.

**WHY NIVASA?**

🏠 ZERO BROKERAGE
Connect directly with property owners. Save thousands on brokerage fees.

🤖 AI-POWERED SEARCH (RIA)
Our AI assistant Ria understands your needs and finds the perfect match.

📍 LOCATION-BASED SEARCH
Find properties near your workplace with commute time calculation.

🔒 TRUST SCORE & SCAM DETECTION
Every listing is scored for trustworthiness. AI detects potential scams.

💬 IN-APP MESSAGING
Chat directly with property owners. No phone number sharing required.

📄 E-CONTRACTS WITH ESIGN
Create legally valid rental agreements with Leegality eSign.

📊 OWNER DASHBOARD
List your property in 60 seconds. Track views and inquiries.
